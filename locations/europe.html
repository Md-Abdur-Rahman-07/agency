Privacy by design

GDPR affects analytics, forms, cookies and hosting choices. We collect only what a feature needs, support consent before non-essential cookies and document where data goes so your team can complete its own assessment.

Accessibility expectations

The European Accessibility Act now applies to many digital services, including online stores. We design towards WCAG guidance and can review an existing site to list practical improvements.

Many languages, one system

Expanding from one country to several is easier when languages, currencies and delivery rules are planned in the structure. We set up language versions with correct tags so search engines show the right one.
