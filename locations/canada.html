Planned for two languages

A bilingual site is more than translated text. Navigation, URLs, forms, emails and search data all need a version in each language. We plan that structure early. French copy can come from your team or a translator you choose.

Accessibility and privacy

Ontario organisations may have duties under the AODA, while PIPEDA, Quebec Law 25 and CASL shape how personal data and email consent are handled. We build the features your advisers ask for and design towards WCAG guidance.

Working across time zones

Calls are scheduled for Eastern through Pacific time. Between them you receive written progress updates and a preview link, so decisions do not wait for the next meeting.
