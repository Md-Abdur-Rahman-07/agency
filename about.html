Our story

Different strengths. One dependable team.

OrbitWick began with a group of engineers who studied together and later developed expertise across design, software development, cybersecurity and infrastructure. We shared a belief that technology should solve real problems, feel intuitive to use and be built to last.

We saw clients having to coordinate separate vendors for design, development and security, often with no one looking after the whole experience. OrbitWick brings those disciplines together in one collaborative studio. From the first conversation through launch and ongoing care, our team keeps the work connected, communicates clearly and takes responsibility for the details.
