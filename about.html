Our story

Built by engineers, for dependable digital products

OrbitWick was founded by a group of engineers who studied together and went on to develop deep expertise in different areas of technology, from interface design and software development to security and infrastructure.

Working with clients, we saw how often a single project was split across several vendors, with design, code and security handled in isolation. We formed OrbitWick to bring those disciplines into one accountable team, so projects are planned, built, secured and supported as a whole.
