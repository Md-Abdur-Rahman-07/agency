E-commerce Checkout Audit: What to Fix Before Buying More Ads

15 November 20263 min read

On this page

  1. Explain the commitment before payment
  2. Remove avoidable form friction
  3. Separate payment from fulfilment
  4. Test the uncomfortable paths
  5. Measure changes against a clear baseline
Explore E-commerce ↗
The short answer

Follow an order all the way from product selection to fulfilment. A payment screen that looks right is not proof that the whole checkout works.

Before increasing your advertising budget, try buying from your own store on a phone. Choose a product, enter an address you genuinely ship to and work through the payment options. Note every point where you hesitate. A checkout audit starts with this ordinary customer task, not a redesign of the payment button.

Abandoned carts have many causes, including visitors who never intended to buy. The useful question is narrower: which obstacles can your store remove, and how will you tell whether the change helped? Work from observed problems rather than a universal conversion promise.

Explain the commitment before payment

Customers should understand what they are buying, the relevant delivery options and the total shown at checkout. Check what happens when their address changes the shipping cost or makes a product unavailable. Do not make the shopper discover an important restriction after spending time completing the form.

Review return and support information from the customer's perspective. Is it easy to find the answer without losing the cart? Avoid unsupported reassurance badges. Clear policies and working contact details are more useful than a row of symbols nobody can verify.

Remove avoidable form friction

W3C's form guidance recommends identifying required information, explaining expected formats and making instructions available to people using assistive technology. Put help near the field that needs it, and make error recovery clear. [1]

Test with a long address, an apartment number, an international phone number and an incorrect postal code. Check whether the error tells the shopper how to recover. Also test keyboard navigation and whether entered details survive a recoverable payment error. These cases reveal issues that a perfect test account misses.

Separate payment from fulfilment

Stripe offers hosted and embedded payment interfaces, so a fully custom card-entry form is not automatically necessary. The appropriate integration depends on your store and payment requirements. [2]

A success page should not be the only trigger for order fulfilment: a customer can pay and lose connectivity before returning to the store. Stripe's fulfilment documentation describes using payment events through webhooks and handling repeated calls safely. Have your developer confirm that a paid order cannot be lost or fulfilled twice. [3]

Test the uncomfortable paths

  • A payment is declined, then the customer retries successfully.
  • The customer closes the browser after paying.
  • The same payment notification arrives more than once.
  • A chosen product becomes unavailable during checkout.
  • A refund or cancellation needs to be reflected in the order record.
  • The customer mistypes an email address and needs help finding the order.

Use the payment provider's testing environment and coordinate any production test with the store owner. Keep test purchases out of operational reporting where possible. The goal is to prove the workflow, not to generate transactions that confuse the team.

Measure changes against a clear baseline

Define the steps you can reliably observe: cart, checkout started, payment attempted and order completed. Look for differences by device and payment method. Make a small set of changes, record the release date and allow enough comparable activity before drawing conclusions.

If the store has little traffic, usability observations and support messages can be more actionable than a noisy percentage. Ask recent customers what nearly stopped them. A specific delivery question can lead to a useful correction even when the analytics sample is small.
