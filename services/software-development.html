Start with the workflow

Good software begins with a process worth improving. We map who does what, which information moves between people and where time is lost. That gives us a first release small enough to finish and useful enough to adopt.

From MVP to a growing product

A first version should prove that people will use it. We build in stages, test with real users and leave room for billing, permissions and integrations as the product grows. You see working software throughout the project, not only at the end.

Security from the first commit

Access rules, input validation, safe authentication and backups are part of the build. Because security is one of our services, the people reviewing risk work beside the people writing code.

Questions and answers

Do you build SaaS products?

Yes. We can help define the first release, design the interface, build the application and prepare it for launch.

Who owns the source code?

Ownership and handover are agreed in writing before work begins, including code, design files and accounts.

Can you take over an existing application?

Yes. We start with a review of the code, hosting and known problems, then recommend what to keep, repair or replace.
