Signs a redesign is due

The site is hard to use on phones, takes too long to load, no longer matches your services or is difficult to update. Enquiries falling while traffic holds steady is another common sign.

Protecting search traffic

Before design begins we record the pages that bring visitors, the links pointing to them and the searches they appear for. Those pages keep their purpose, and changed addresses receive permanent redirects.

Improve in stages

Not every site needs a full rebuild. Sometimes new templates, better content and faster images solve most of the problem. We recommend the smallest change that meets the goal.

Questions and answers

Will a redesign hurt my rankings?

Any large change carries some risk. Careful redirects, preserved content and checks after launch reduce it considerably.

Can you keep my current content?

Yes. We can keep it, edit it or restructure it after reviewing which pages are useful.

Can you redesign a WordPress site?

Yes, including sites built with page builders or older themes.
