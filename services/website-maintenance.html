Why maintenance matters

Most website problems build up quietly: an outdated plugin, a failed backup, a form that stopped sending. Regular checks catch them before customers do.

What a care plan covers

Plans are based on your platform and how often the site changes. We agree the tasks, response expectations and included time in writing, so you know what is covered.

Taking over from another developer

We start with access, hosting, backups and a health check. You receive a list of urgent issues and recommended improvements before regular care begins.

What's included

  • Software and plugin updates
  • Regular backups
  • Security updates
  • Bug fixes and small changes
  • Uptime and performance monitoring
  • Technical support

Questions and answers

Do you maintain sites you did not build?

Yes, after an initial review of the site, hosting and access.

Monthly or yearly?

Both are available. A monthly plan is a simple way to begin.

Does maintenance include new features?

Small changes can be included. Larger features are scoped and quoted separately.
