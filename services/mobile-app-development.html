Native or cross-platform?

Cross-platform frameworks share most code between iOS and Android, which suits many business apps. Native development is worth considering when an app depends heavily on device hardware or platform-specific behaviour. We advise based on your features and budget.

Prototype before code

A clickable prototype lets you test the main journeys on a real phone before development begins. Changing a screen at this stage is far cheaper than changing finished code.

Life after launch

Operating systems, devices and store rules change every year. We plan for updates, crash monitoring and small improvements so the app stays dependable.

Questions and answers

Do I need a mobile app or a website?

If people need your service often, offline or with notifications, an app may help. If they mostly find you through search, start with a strong mobile website.

Can you publish the app for us?

Yes. We prepare store listings and builds, and submit through developer accounts that belong to your business.

Can the app use our existing data?

Usually, yes. If your system has an API we connect to it. If not, we can discuss building one.
