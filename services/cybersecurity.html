Cybersecurity services in plain language.

We review your website or application, explain the risks clearly and help fix what matters most.

Get a free consultationSee our work

Know what is exposed

A review looks at login and access rules, forms, APIs, third-party components, hosting configuration and data handling. Testing is agreed in writing first, including what is in scope and when it happens.

Findings you can act on

Each finding explains what could happen, how serious it is and how to fix it. Your priorities are clear whether our team or yours does the work.

Fix and retest

A report alone does not reduce risk. We can correct the issues, confirm the fixes and recommend simple routines that stop them returning.

What's included

  • Website security audits
  • Vulnerability assessments
  • API security review
  • Security hardening
  • Secure configuration
  • Vulnerability remediation

Questions and answers

How often should a website be reviewed?

Before launch, after major changes and at regular intervals that match the sensitivity of your data.

Will testing disrupt my site?

We agree methods and timing beforehand and can work on a staging copy where suitable.

Can you help after a hack?

Yes. Contact us with what you know. We can help assess, clean and harden the site.
