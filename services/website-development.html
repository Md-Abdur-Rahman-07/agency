What makes a website worth building

A useful website answers three questions quickly: what you offer, why someone should trust you and how to get in touch. We settle those answers before design begins, so every page has a job. Navigation, headings and calls to action follow from what your customers are looking for.

Custom code or a CMS?

WordPress suits teams that publish often and want to edit pages themselves. Custom code suits portals, unusual layouts and sites connected to other systems. We explain the trade-offs in cost, speed and maintenance, then recommend the simpler option that meets your needs.

Built to be found

Search visibility starts with the build. We use clear headings, descriptive titles, clean URLs, structured data, compressed images and a mobile-first layout. If you are replacing an existing site, we map old addresses to new ones so existing search traffic is planned for.

Questions and answers

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of page types, content, integrations and design work. We give you a written scope and quote after a short conversation, so you can compare it fairly with others.

How long does a website take?

A focused business site is usually quicker than a portal or store. Content readiness is the most common cause of delay. Your proposal includes a timeline with milestones.

Will I be able to edit the website?

Yes, if you want to. We can build on a CMS and show your team how to update pages, images and articles.
