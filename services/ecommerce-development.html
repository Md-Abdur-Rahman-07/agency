Choosing the right platform

Shopify reduces technical upkeep. WooCommerce offers more control on your own hosting. A custom storefront makes sense when your catalogue or buying process does not fit either. We compare running costs as well as build costs.

Fewer lost carts

Unexpected costs, forced accounts and confusing forms make shoppers leave. We keep delivery, returns and payment information visible, shorten the checkout and test it on real phones.

Ready for search

Category structure, unique product descriptions, product data, image sizes and internal links help search engines understand your store. We set these foundations during the build.

Questions and answers

Can you move my store to Shopify?

Yes. We plan products, customers, content and redirects before the switch, and test the new store before it goes live.

Which payment methods can you add?

It depends on your country and platform. We confirm suitable providers for your market during planning.

Can my team manage products?

Yes. We set up the admin area and provide handover guidance for products, orders and content.
